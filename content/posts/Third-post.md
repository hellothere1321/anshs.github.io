It's been a while since I last updated my blog, and I never did post the update on the Crowley and Aziraphale classifier I started making. While I did make progress on the actual machine learning classifier itself I realized that I was too time bound to finish the project especially with a big important event coming up, but more on that later. Working on the module was a very interesting test in both machine learning, and linguistic patterns associated with it. In the end even though I didn't get as far as training and testing my model, the project produced something a lot more important then that.

While working on the project one night, I realized that this wasn't where I wanted to be, I didn't want to do something that has already been done to death for the sake of it, and instead I wanted to actually test out what I have learnt in actually helping people. 

That's where the idea came in, I thought about it a lot during my project work, but how do people communicate? How does a classifier extend to the real world and that's when it clicked, I wanted to make a classifier in real life. It wouldn't classify voices because that's been done, but classify the very linguistic intention of a person, breaking through bounds of any traditional form of AAC (Augmentative and alternative communication), and going from slow gaze based, or keyboard based techniques to a system that understands them linguistically to a deeper level thus allowing it to be faster then any system seen before. 

As an application for science fair to showcase this idea, I want to investigate how I can expand current systems and optimizations so it will require less effort for better understanding of what the user wants to express, whether it be through LLM assisted writing or models that develop around the person who is using them. 

In the end I still haven't quite got my approach down but I think this is shaping up to be a very interesting project and hopefully within a week or two I'll have a prototype to show you guys.

Language is one of our biggest connectors to others, and everyone deserves to understand it to its fullest, so I hope this project comes to fruition, anyway see y'all soon!
