I've spent a large part of my life travelling, from India to Singapore, and later Chicago. Moving around has also meant travelling through a multitude of languages, dialects and ways of speaking. The accents in India, indeed my accent when I lived there was vastly different from the American way of speaking, and the fact that all of it can be recognized as the same language even with vastly different sounds is fascinating to me. A lot of that has been reflected in the way I talk, and its especially striking to me when I look at videos from the past given I sound not only younger but like a different person with a different accent and even way of speech.

While that may be one of the reasons I'm interested in linguistics, a large part of my interest comes from my love of reading and books. I've always been curious about how authors manage to use a limited palette of words to create worlds, with differing characters; and sometimes hardest of all similar characters that have to be made distinct from the millions of characters that have already been written. Another of my interests within writing lies in how characters are interpreted differently, and how they evoke different reactions from different people. Another question I have is why people read the same characters of text on a piece of paper and imagine characters that are vastly different and what does drive it. 

Lets back track a bit, My name is Ansh and I'm currently a resident of Chicago but have spent most of my childhood in India and later Singapore. I wanted to document my journey in linguistics because as a form of self motivation but also as a way to put my thoughts on the subject and my learning out in to the world. This blog is going to be a weekly journal of what I've learned about linguistics and also to put my interest into the world. 

I don't really know where my passion will take me; sometimes I may focus on books and writing, others might be about regional dialects and accents, or even something that is completely random and unconnected to both. A big part of the reason that I made this blog is to try and keep a record of my growth along my journey and understand what I want to do.
